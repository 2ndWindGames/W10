Keep a note of when you first opened it.

Opened Note records opening dates for everyday products such as milk, sauces and cosmetics. This release has a Korean-language interface.

• Record each product separately, including products with the same name.
• See elapsed calendar days since opening.
• Optionally choose one reminder date and time. Android battery settings may delay delivery.
• Add one photo, a short note and a category.
• Sort by overdue reminders, upcoming reminders or recent opening dates.
• Mark a product as finished to archive its record and cancel its reminder.
• Restore an archived record without replaying past reminders.
• Use larger text and delete all local records and photos in Settings.

The free version supports up to 15 active products. Archived records remain available to view, edit and delete. There are no ads, purchases, accounts or automatic cloud backups.

Records and selected photos stay in the app's internal storage. The app does not send them to a server. Uninstalling the app or changing devices does not preserve your records.

Reminder dates are dates you choose for checking a product. Opened Note does not calculate expiry dates or assess whether a product is safe to eat or use. Follow product labeling and storage instructions.

Requires Android 8.0 or later.
